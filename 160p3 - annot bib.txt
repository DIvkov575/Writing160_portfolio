Proposal


I want to create an infographic/website  that visualizes the relationship between rising tourist numbers at Lake Atitlán and measurable environmental decline (using statistics like water transparency, oxygen levels, and cyanobacteria outbreaks). I have plans to travel to Guatemala and am curious about the country, so I want to understand the possible environmental pressures on one of its most iconic destinations before I visit. Questions I hope to answer: How have specific water quality metrics changed over the past few decades? Is there a measurable correlation between tourism peaks and pollution spikes? What is projected for the lake's future if current trends continue?


Thesis
Tourism growth at Lake Atitlán, Guatemala has outpaced the region's water treatment infrastructure, driving eutrophication of the lake and destabilizing the lakeshore communities that depend on its resources.
